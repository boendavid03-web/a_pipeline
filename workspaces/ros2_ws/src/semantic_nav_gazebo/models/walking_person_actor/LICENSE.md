# Walking actor asset notice

`model.sdf` uses the package-local animated mesh at
`../person_standing/meshes/walk.dae`. Its existing `model.config` attributes
the source character to Marina Kollmitz and MakeHuman. MakeHuman Community
releases its core graphical assets under CC0-1.0; this package keeps the asset
local and does not download it at runtime.

The original attribution is retained even though CC0 does not require it.
